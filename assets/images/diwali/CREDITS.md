# Diwali photos: credits

The twelve photographs in this folder are used on the Diwali 2026 theme of
www.myneedurban.com. Every one is either in the public domain (CC0 1.0) or
licensed CC BY 2.0, which allows commercial use with credit. They were found
through [Openverse](https://openverse.org) and filtered to those two licences
only (no "share alike" or "non-commercial" photos).

Changes made to every photo: resized, cropped by the page layout and
recompressed to WebP. Nothing else was altered.

## Not photographs

The sky lanterns, fireworks, embers, petals, diyas, rangoli, garlands and
cursors are drawn in code (`js/diwali-art.js`, `js/diwali-fx.js`). The greeting
card is drawn on a canvas in the visitor's own browser (`js/diwali-card.js`).
